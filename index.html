Fasting Instructions

Do not eat solid food after midnight. You may drink clear liquids (water, apple juice, black coffee) up to 2 hours before your arrival time.

The Day Before
Shower with antibacterial soap
Use CHG (chlorhexidine) soap if provided — do not apply lotions or deodorant after
Remove nail polish & jewelry
Pulse oximeters read through your fingernails during surgery
Pack your hospital bag
Loose clothing, slip-on shoes, ID, insurance card, and comfort items
Confirm arrival time & location
Call the surgical center to double-check your check-in time and parking
Begin fasting at midnight
No food, candy, or gum. Clear liquids only until 2 hours before arrival

What to Bring

Documents

  • Photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  • Insurance card and referral forms
  • Signed consent and advance directive
  • List of current medications

Personal Items

  • Loose, comfortable going-home clothes
  • Slip-on shoes (no laces)
  • Phone and charger
  • A book or something to pass the time

Leave at Home

  • Jewelry, watches, and piercings
  • Valuables and large amounts of cash
  • Contact lenses (bring glasses instead)
  • Dentures and hearing aids (bring cases)
💊
Medication Reminder

Do not stop or change any prescribed medication without your surgeon's approval. Blood thinners, diabetes medications, and blood pressure drugs may need special timing.
